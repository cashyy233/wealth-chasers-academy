Contract and lot notes

  • NQ is generally $20 per point; MNQ $2 per point. With a 0.25-point tick, that is generally $5 and $0.50 per tick respectively.
  • ES is generally $50 per point; MES $5 per point. Verify current exchange/broker specifications.
  • Forex pip value depends on currency pair, lot size, and account currency. Enter the verified pip value for one full lot and select a suitable increment.
  • Size rounds down to keep estimated risk within budget. Fees, slippage, gaps, and fast markets may increase losses.
Risk formula

Risk budget = account balance × risk %.

Estimated size = risk budget ÷ (stop distance × point/pip value + costs), rounded down.
