Lesson 1 • Day 1

Read a candlestick

A candle summarizes price movement during a chosen time period. The body shows the open-to-close range; the wick shows the high and low reached.

Bearish

Four things each candle tells you

  • Open: the price when the candle began.
  • Close: the price when the candle ended.
  • High: the highest price reached.
  • Low: the lowest price reached.

Beginner rules

  • A green candle commonly means its close was above its open.
  • A red candle commonly means its close was below its open.
  • A candle color alone does not predict the next candle.
  • Try 5-minute and 15-minute charts to see how timeframes change what each candle represents.
Practice: Open TradingView, search for the E-mini Nasdaq-100 futures symbol, and identify 10 candles. Label each bullish or bearish. This is chart practice, not a trade signal.
